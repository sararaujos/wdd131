SecondChance Kids: Site Planning Document

Sara Araujo — WDD 131

Site Name

Name: SecondChance Kids: Curated Pre-Loved Baby & Toddler Store

Reasoning: Babies and toddlers grow out of clothing incredibly fast, often leaving items perfectly intact. "SecondChance Kids" reflects the eco-friendly mission of giving these premium garments a second life while helping parents save money and reduce textile waste.

Site Purpose

The purpose of SecondChance Kids is to offer a vibrant, modern, and high-contrast pastel marketplace for curated, high-quality secondhand clothing and accessories for children aged 0 to 5. The platform aims to make sustainable parenting trendy and budget-friendly, featuring a dynamic product catalog grid and an interactive JavaScript Sustainability Calculator that tracks the water and carbon footprints saved by choosing pre-loved kids' wear.

Target Audience & Scenarios

The target audience consists of modern, eco-conscious parents, family members, and gift-shoppers looking for clean, premium, and affordable toddler fashion without supporting fast-fashion waste.

  • Scenario 1: "My 2-year-old outgrew all their winter clothes in three months. I want to quickly filter and find a curated bundle of premium jackets and pants by age range without scrolling through adult clothing."
  • Scenario 2: "I want to use the dynamic calculator to show my family how many thousands of liters of water we saved this year by swapping and buying pre-loved items for our newborn."

Color Schema

To capture a playful, modern, and energetic children's retail marketplace, the site uses a high-contrast palette of bright pastels wrapped in bold UI elements:

  • Primary Background: #FFF2B2 (Bright Pastel Yellow) - Joyful and energetic, used as the primary background color for the store experience.
  • Secondary Accent: #BAE1FF (Pastel Sky Blue) - Used for primary navigational bars, tabs, and interactive buttons.
  • Accent Highlight: #FFC6FF (Pastel Pink) - Used for hover states, special promo tags, and focus indicators.
  • Calculator & Safety Accent: #BFFCC6 (Pastel Mint Green) - Used specifically to highlight the Sustainability Impact Calculator section and success elements.
  • Typography & Structural Borders: #111111 (Sharp Black) - Creates a trendy, modern comic/marketplace aesthetic with sharp outlines.

Typography

  • Heading Font: "Plus Jakarta Sans" (or Helvetica, sans-serif)
    A thick, rounded, geometric sans-serif used in uppercase for major headings to look playful yet highly professional and clean.
  • Body Font: "Inter" (or Arial, sans-serif)
    A very legible, clean modern UI font used for product descriptions, sizing grids, and input elements in the calculator.
