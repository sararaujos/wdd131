Project References and Citations

This page documents the resources and materials leveraged during the building of TinyTreasure.

  • Design Inspiration: UI elements and flat shadow patterns inspired by Depop, Sellpy, and regional retail apps.
  • Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter loaded from the official Google Fonts API.
  • Image Assets: Optimized standard open-source vector placeholders from Wikimedia Commons used for responsive visual testing.
  • Environmental Impact Data: Generic water and carbon calculations adapted from global apparel sustainability metrics.
